Auto-fill clears what you typed and splits each version’s budget using the recommended mix. Each channel only runs in the markets that fit it: search, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit and retargeting everywhere except China; CTV, partners, newsletters and podcasts in North America, Europe and Australia; WeChat and Baidu in China; Naver in Korea. Every placement belongs to one marketplace side (retailers, brands or win-back), and the split in section 2 sets how much each side gets.

9 · Creative units by platform · specs
Check a unit to take its whole package (every size), or click sizes one by one. A dash in the box means only some sizes are picked; click it to pick them all again.
Ticked units and sizes go into the Spec Sheet and the BOM. “Used in N media placements” counts the media buys that run a unit, not its sizes; untick any size you won’t produce. Units with the same spec on a platform are listed once. Unticking a unit removes it; placements left with no units drop out and their budget moves to the remaining placements.
10 · Messaging & ad copy fills the BOM
One line per option. Each BOM master uses the copy for the initiative that carries most of its budget, and picks lines that fit its character limits. Write a mix of short and long lines so search and display have options.
